This update is all about battery life, without giving up what NagRX promises: the alarm keeps nagging, out loud, until you take your medication, even when the phone is locked and the ringer is off.

- Alarms now wake the phone only at the moment they need to ring, instead of checking the clock every second all day.
- The silent background stream that keeps alarms reliable now uses the lightest audio settings iOS allows.
- The haptic engine powers down between alarms.
- The widget updates only when your medication status actually changes, instead of refreshing every few minutes.
- The medication list is sent to your Apple Watch only when it has changed.
