NagRX now uses real system alarms.

- Doses ring with the same kind of alarm as the Clock app, so they sound even when your iPhone is on silent or in a Focus, and NagRX no longer has to keep running in the background. That's much easier on your battery.
- Your reminder timing is unchanged: if you don't take a dose, it keeps ringing again at your chosen interval.
- Tap Stop on the alarm when you've taken it. Tap Snooze to be reminded again in 15 minutes.
- New Live Activity: a snoozed dose shows a countdown in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.
- The widget now turns red right at the dose time, on its own.
- Tidier notifications: only the newest one stays in Notification Center, and it clears itself after 30 minutes.
